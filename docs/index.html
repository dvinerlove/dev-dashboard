01 // CRASH HANDLING

Restarts what crashes.

Exponential backoff: 2, 5, 10, 20 seconds, reset after a minute of uptime. Five crashes inside sixty seconds and the circuit breaker trips instead of burning your CPU. A manual start resets everything.

02 // PORT ATTRIBUTION

Every port, owned.

Every TCP listener on the machine, attributed to the service that owns it. Conflicts get detected before they bite, then remapped automatically by scanning +20.
