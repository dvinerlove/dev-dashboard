## Key verbatim quotes

- TL;DR: "Themes went from 98 hand-set variables to three inputs (base colour, accent colour, contrast), generated in the LCH colour space."
- "The redesign cut chrome: less blue in the neutrals, calmer sidebar, tabs and headers, and stronger contrast for content."
- Elevation: "Co-founder Karri Saarinen explored concepts in Figma, mostly using opacities of black and white to set elevation and hierarchy."
- Hierarchy technique: "Use brightness steps for hierarchy. On a dark UI, white, grey and dim grey do more than extra font sizes or colours."
- Type: "Inter Display for headings... regular Inter for everything else."
- Prescription: "Pick a base, an accent and a contrast level, and derive every surface in a perceptual space like LCH or OKLCH."
- Rollout: "stress tests, a feature flag, a private beta and a gradual rollout" in ~6 weeks.
