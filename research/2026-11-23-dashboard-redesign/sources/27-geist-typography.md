## Key verbatim quotes

- Mechanism: "The classes below pre-set a combination of `font-size`, `line-height`, `letter-spacing`, and `font-weight` for you based on the Geist Core Figma system."
- Heading scale: text-heading-{72,64,56,48,40,32,24,20,16,14}; Buttons: text-button-16 (largest), text-button-14 (default), text-button-12 (tiny, inside input fields).
- Label styles (dense-UI core): "Label 14 — Most common text style of all. Used in many menus."; "Label 13 ... Used as a secondary line next to other labels. Tabular is used when conveying numbers for consistent spacing."; "Label 12 — Used for tertiary level text in busy views."
- Mono pairings: "Label 14 Mono — Largest form of mono, to pair with larger (>14) text."; "Label 13 Mono — Used to pair with Label 14."; "Copy 13 Mono — Used for inline code mentions."
- Copy styles: "Copy 14 — Most commonly used text style."; "Copy 13 — For secondary text and views where space is a premium."
- Scale floor: smallest styles are 12px (Label 12, Button 12, Copy 13) — Geist never goes below 12px for UI text.
