## Verbatim values (sRGB fallbacks; P3 variants also shipped)

`.dark, .dark-theme {`
- `--gray-1: #111111;` (app background)
- `--gray-2: #191919;` (subtle bg / surface)
- `--gray-3: #222222;` (component bg)
- `--gray-4: #2a2a2a;` (hover)
- `--gray-5: #313131;` (active/pressed)
- `--gray-6: #3a3a3a;` (border: subtle separators)
- `--gray-7: #484848;` (border: UI element)
- `--gray-8: #606060;` (border: hover/strong)
- `--gray-9: #6e6e6e;` (solid bg / accent step)
- `--gray-10: #7b7b7b;` (hover solid)
- `--gray-11: #b4b4b4;` (secondary text/icons)
- `--gray-12: #eeeeee;` (primary text)
`}`

(Radix documents steps 1–2 as backgrounds, 3–5 component backgrounds, 6–8 borders, 9–10 solids, 11–12 text — matching the Geist 10-step role mapping.)
